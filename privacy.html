This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through MasterBaller's website, accounts, and services.

We may use collected information to:

MasterBaller uses AI technology to provide soccer-related evaluations, coaching feedback, and personalized recommendations.

These providers may process submitted information according to their applicable privacy policies, terms, and data-processing settings. Their retention and model-training practices may differ.

8. Children's Privacy

MasterBaller is intended for athletes of different ages, and we take the privacy of younger users seriously.

Children under 13 may not create accounts or submit personal information unless and until MasterBaller implements an appropriate parental consent process and obtains verifiable parental consent where required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or other applicable laws.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to:

  • Ask whether information about their child has been collected.
  • Request access to their child's personal information.
  • Request deletion of their child's information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

If we discover that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected without legally required consent, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue, including deletion where required.

Users aged 13–17 should involve a parent or legal guardian when sharing personal information or using paid services.
