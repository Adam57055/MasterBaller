Frequently Asked Questions

What is MasterBaller?

MasterBaller is a soccer tech software designed to evaluate a player's skill to see what needs improvements. Players of all skill levels are welcome.

Do I have to pay?

No! We don't charge you for anything. Since this is a Beta version, we want to gain users that have a passion for soccer and want to improve.

I'm running into a bug. What should I do?

You should go to our support page and fill out a ticket. We'll get back to you as soon as possible.
