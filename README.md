# Overview
MasterBaller is an AI-assisted startup designed to help aspiring soccer athletes achieve their full potential. The software asks questions based on the player's skill level.
If the player is completely new and aspiring, it will estimate their skill and suggest what position they should play and exercises to thrive in that position.


# AI
The AI developed will assess the player's capabilities in multiple areas of soccer, including speed, stamina, and strength, to get a better sense of what position suits them. They will have a full recommendation and breakdown of how they should approach the positions. They will have a single best-ranked choice followed by similar positions they can play. Once a position is chosen, they will begin their journey to achieve the best skill they can get in that position.
